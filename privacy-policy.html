Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

1. Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Watermark Studio (“the website”, “we”) handles information when you use the website's five pages: the watermark tool, About, Contact, Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions. It describes the website as it is actually built today. If the website changes in a way that affects your privacy, this policy will be updated.

2. Files selected by users

The website lets you select PDF, JPG, PNG and WebP files. Your selected files are read by your own browser. The website's code does not upload them, store them in a database, or send them to us.

3. Browser-side file processing

Watermarks are applied on your device. PDFs are processed in memory by the PDF-Lib JavaScript library (and previewed with pdf.js), and images are processed with the browser's HTML canvas. The watermarked result is created in memory and offered to you as a download. When you close or reload the page, the in-memory copies are discarded by your browser. The website does not save your files, settings or watermark text in cookies, local storage or similar browser storage.

Because processing happens on your device, the file stays subject to the security of your own device, browser, extensions and downloads folder.

4. Information you provide through email

If you contact us at heycoders.anurag@gmail.com, we receive whatever you choose to include: your email address, name, message, and any attachments. We use this only to read and reply to your message, to investigate bugs and to consider suggestions. The contact form on the Contact page does not submit anything to a server; it opens a draft in your own email application, and nothing is sent unless you send it. Your email is handled by your email provider and by Gmail, which have their own privacy practices. Please avoid sending confidential documents.

5. Server logs and hosting-provider data

The website is a static site delivered by a hosting provider. Hosting providers commonly record technical request data, such as IP address, date and time, requested page, browser and device type, and referring page, for security and operation. We do not control the details of this logging, which depend on the provider used to host the site. Visiting the website therefore does involve some automatic processing of technical data, even though your files are not uploaded.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

The website's own code does not set cookies and does not use local storage or session storage. Third-party services that deliver the website's code (see section 9), or the hosting provider, may set cookies or process similar identifiers according to their own policies. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

7. Analytics

No analytics service is configured in the current version of the website. If analytics are added in the future, this section will be updated to name the service and explain what it collects.

8. Advertising and Google AdSense

No advertising, including Google AdSense, is configured in the current version of the website, and the website does not display advertisements. If advertising is added later, this policy will be updated before ads appear, to describe the provider, cookies used and how to manage ad personalisation.

9. Third-party libraries and services

To work, the website loads code from public content delivery networks (CDNs):

  • Tailwind CSS (styling) from cdn.tailwindcss.com.
  • PDF-Lib (PDF watermarking) from cdn.jsdelivr.net, with unpkg.com as a fallback.
  • pdf.js (drawing the page 1 preview of a PDF) from cdnjs.cloudflare.com, with cdn.jsdelivr.net as a fallback. It reads your PDF in your browser only.

When your browser requests these files, the CDN operators can receive standard technical request data such as your IP address, browser type and the page that requested the file. This is how CDNs normally work. We do not send your files or watermark settings to these services. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies.

10. Data security limitations

Processing files in your browser avoids sending them over the internet to us, which reduces exposure. However, no system is perfectly secure. The website relies on third-party code loaded from CDNs, on your browser and on your device. Malware, malicious browser extensions or a compromised device can access files regardless of this website. Use the website on a trusted device, keep your browser updated, and avoid processing highly sensitive files on shared or public computers.

11. Data retention considerations

We do not retain your selected files, because we do not receive them. Emails you send us are kept for as long as needed to respond and to keep a record of bug reports or suggestions, and you may ask us to delete them (see section 13). Hosting providers and CDNs keep their own logs under their own retention schedules, which we do not control. Watermarked files you download are stored wherever your browser saves downloads, and you are responsible for managing them.

12. Children's privacy

The website is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us by email, please contact us and we will delete it.

13. User choices and privacy rights

You choose which files to select and whether to email us. Depending on where you live, privacy laws may give you rights over personal information held about you, such as the right to access, correct or delete it. For information we hold (which is limited to emails you send us), you can request access or deletion by writing to the address in section 15. Rights relating to hosting-provider or CDN logs must generally be exercised with those providers. You can also limit data collection by using browser privacy settings or tracking-prevention tools.

14. Policy updates

We may update this policy when the website or its services change. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it was last revised. Continued use of the website after changes means you accept the updated policy.

15. Contact information

For privacy questions or requests, email heycoders.anurag@gmail.com or visit the Contact page. See also the Terms and Conditions and the Watermark Tool.
