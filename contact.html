Contact

Questions, bug reports and ideas are welcome. Watermark Studio is maintained by an individual developer, so replies may take some time.

Email

The best way to reach us is by email: heycoders.anurag@gmail.com.

Please do not attach confidential documents unless they are necessary to explain a problem. If you can reproduce a bug with a harmless sample file, that is much safer for you.

Reporting a bug

The more detail you provide, the easier it is to find and fix a problem. Useful information includes:

  • What you were trying to do (for example, “watermark a 12-page PDF with a repeating pattern”).
  • The file type and approximate size.
  • The watermark settings you used (text, size, opacity, rotation, spacing, pattern).
  • What you expected to happen and what actually happened, including any error message shown on the page.
  • Your device, operating system and browser with its version (for example, Chrome 130 on Windows 11, or Safari on iPhone).
  • Whether the problem happens every time, and whether it also happens in another browser.

Send a suggestion or feedback

Use the form below to prepare an email. This form does not send anything from the website. When you press the button, your own email app opens with a draft addressed to heycoders.anurag@gmail.com, and you decide whether to send it. If no email app opens, copy the address above and write to us manually.
